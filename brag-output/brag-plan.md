# Brag Plan: legalBuddy

## What is this app?

legalBuddy is a lavender legal-reading workspace: paste an agreement, get a plain-language finding, jump to the exact sentence, and download a brief for a lawyer.

## The angle

The contract already contains the sentence that matters. legalBuddy does not invent a verdict. It shows you that sentence, checks the quote, and hands you a brief. The video is a quiet product film of that proof, not a generic AI assistant ad.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

Lavender canvas. Tiny eyebrow: **LESS LEGAL JARGON. MORE UNDERSTANDING.** Then the product H1, full frame: **Before you sign.** The consult illustration from the dashboard sits on the right so this is the real app, not a restyle.

## Key moments (the middle)

- A freelancer agreement card appears. A cursor considers **Explore the sample**, then presses **Make it clear**.
- The finding **You could hand over ownership before being paid** arrives. The source quote is highlighted in place: *All intellectual property rights in the deliverables transfer to the Client upon creation…*
- Status chip: **Quotations matched to source text.**

## Outro / punchline

**Download brief.** Then the legalBuddy wordmark. Small, honest closer: **Information and preparation only. Not legal advice.**

## User flow worth showing

Explore the sample → Make it clear → open the ownership finding and see the quote in the document → Download brief.

## Tone

- Preset: `polished`
- Direction: Quiet lavender dashboard film. Restraint is the joke: the AI has to show the sentence.
- Voice: No narration. Product copy only.
- Pacing: Four scenes, 4–6s each. Soft slides, no slam.
- Typography: Instrument Serif for headlines, DM Sans for UI. Mixed case. Generous hold.
- Transitions: Soft crossfade / slide.

## Format and duration

Landscape 1920×1080. **20.0 seconds.**

What happens to your text

Files are read on your device. PDF files are converted to text locally; only the extracted text is sent for analysis. A live review sends the document, reader context, and any question or revised version through Margin’s server to Google Gemini after you consent.

Margin does not create accounts, save documents to a database, or store them in browser storage. The current workspace lives in your tab’s memory. Reloading or clearing the workspace removes it from the app. Downloads are saved on your device and must be deleted separately.

Processing outside your device

Google processes live requests under its Gemini API terms and data-use policies. These can vary by account and billing tier. In particular, unpaid services may use submitted content to improve products. Do not submit confidential, privileged, personal, or sensitive information. Replace names and identifying details before processing. Margin cannot guarantee legal privilege or zero retention by infrastructure providers.

Read the Gemini API terms. Vercel hosts the application and can process connection metadata such as IP addresses and request timing. Margin does not intentionally log document bodies or AI responses. Its short-lived rate limiter stores hashed network identifiers in server memory.

The sample is a walkthrough

The sample agreement is fictional. Its explanations, comparison, and guided answers are curated examples, visibly labeled in the app. They do not make an AI request and are not personalized to your circumstances.

Information, not legal advice

Margin explains supplied wording and helps prepare questions for a qualified legal professional. It does not research current law, determine enforceability, recommend signing, create a lawyer-client relationship, or replace a lawyer. A matching quote shows where wording came from; it does not prove that the explanation is complete or legally correct.

AI can miss clauses, misunderstand facts, and make mistakes. Read the full document, retain all versions, and ask a qualified local lawyer about decisions, deadlines, disputes, or urgent matters. Missing jurisdiction or referenced attachments can materially change an interpretation.

Supported documents

UTF-8 TXT and Markdown, and text-based PDF files up to 2 MB and 30 pages. Each document must contain between 80 and 40,000 characters. Images and scanned PDFs need OCR elsewhere. Password-protected or unreadable files may fail extraction.

Security reports

Report a vulnerability privately through GitHub private vulnerability reporting. Please do not include real legal documents in a public issue.
